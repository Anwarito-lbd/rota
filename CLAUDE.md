# Rota: notes for agents

**Product principle: Rota is where people rent out their clothes. It is not a social network.**

Posts, stories and follows exist only to show pieces and lead to a rental. When choosing between options, pick the one that helps someone rent or lend a piece. Keep the social and privacy UI light, and make the rental flows best-in-class: listing, discovery, availability, booking, messaging about a piece, trust.

- Every account and every story is public; there are no private accounts.
- Keep the safety tools: block, report, message requests, mute.
- The owner writes in French. Answer in French and keep app copy French-first, with EN and ES translations.
- Never push to `main`. Open a PR and let the owner merge it.

For setup, see `README.md`. For security rules, see `docs/superpowers/specs/` and the migration headers in `supabase/migrations/`.
